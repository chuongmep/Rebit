# Hiring and Organization Risk Register

## Scoring model

Likelihood and impact each scored 1 to 5. Risk score = likelihood * impact.

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Score | Owner | Mitigation |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| R1 | Critical role hiring delay | 5 | 5 | 25 | VP Eng + Head Recruiting | Exec sourcing sprint, retained search, compensation flexibility |
| R2 | Scope creep from stakeholder pressure | 5 | 5 | 25 | CTO + CPO | Scope change control board, DRI approvals |
| R3 | Interview inconsistency and false negatives | 4 | 4 | 16 | VP Eng | Calibration sessions, scorecard audits |
| R4 | Burnout from compressed timeline | 4 | 5 | 20 | People Ops + Directors | Capacity guardrails, no-crunch enforcement |
| R5 | Architecture drift across teams | 4 | 5 | 20 | Principal Council | ADR system, weekly architecture review |
| R6 | Offer decline due to market competition | 4 | 4 | 16 | Recruiting Lead | Faster close plan, executive sponsor for finalists |
| R7 | Onboarding inefficiency | 3 | 4 | 12 | People Ops + IDP lead | 2-week onboarding plan and buddy system |
| R8 | Dependency bottlenecks between teams | 4 | 4 | 16 | Program Management | Dependency board and integration milestones |

## Review cadence

1. Weekly risk review for R1 to R5.
2. Monthly full risk register update.
3. Immediate escalation for any score increase above 20.
