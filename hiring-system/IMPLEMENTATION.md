# Rebit Hiring System Implementation Guide

## Purpose

This package turns the master plan into an operating system your team can run immediately.

## What this includes

1. Weekly operating cadence for hiring and technical decisions.
2. Month-by-month hiring execution plan for the 6-month window.
3. Interview system, scorecards, and role templates.
4. Funnel model and dashboard templates.
5. Risk controls and escalation paths.

## Roles and ownership

| Area | Owner | Backup | Cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hiring strategy and priorities | CTO + VP Engineering | CPO | Weekly |
| Recruiting operations | Head of Recruiting | Recruiting Lead | Daily |
| Interview quality | VP Engineering | Director Core Engines | Weekly |
| Funnel analytics and KPI | Recruiting Ops | Finance | Weekly |
| Budget controls | Finance | CEO | Monthly |
| Offer close and acceptance | Hiring Manager + Exec sponsor | Recruiting Lead | Per offer |

## Day 0 to Day 14 implementation steps

1. Confirm target headcount by role family and month.
2. Assign a Directly Responsible Individual for each critical role.
3. Publish and approve role scorecards before sourcing starts.
4. Launch weekly hiring review and 15-minute daily recruiting standup.
5. Set interviewer training and calibration for all panelists.
6. Start executive outbound for top 20 critical candidates.
7. Lock interview SLAs and implement dashboard tracking.

## Weekly operating cadence

| Meeting | Duration | Participants | Output |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Daily recruiting standup | 15 min | Recruiters, HM delegates | Blockers removed, candidate actions assigned |
| Weekly hiring review | 60 min | CTO, VP Eng, Heads of Product/Design, Recruiting | Open roles status, funnel health, decisions |
| Weekly architecture and hiring sync | 45 min | Directors, Principal engineers, Recruiting lead | Priority shifts and role requirement updates |
| Offer review | 30 min | Hiring manager, recruiter, finance | Offer package approval and close plan |

## SLA policy

1. Resume review by hiring manager: less than 24 hours.
2. Candidate feedback submission: same business day.
3. Interview to decision: less than 72 hours.
4. Offer creation after final yes: less than 48 hours.

## Success metrics

1. Time to fill by role family.
2. Offer acceptance rate by team.
3. Pass-through rates by interview stage.
4. Interviewer utilization and response SLA.
5. New hire 60-day retention and performance signal.

## How to use this folder

1. Start with 01_roadmap_6_months.md.
2. Set up weekly workflow using 02_operating_cadence.md.
3. Use templates from templates/ for each role.
4. Track in dashboards/hiring_dashboard_template.csv.
5. Run risk checks from 06_risk_register.md every week.
6. Execute crate ownership and role tasks from 08_rust_architecture_and_role_tasks.md.
