# Hiring Operating Cadence

## Weekly structure

### Monday

1. Executive hiring review (60 min).
2. Critical role status updates and blockers.
3. Offer strategy for active finalists.

### Tuesday

1. Interview calibration panel (45 min).
2. Review scorecard consistency and false-negative risks.

### Wednesday

1. Sourcing deep-dive (45 min).
2. Talent market map updates and outbound list refresh.

### Thursday

1. Team staffing and dependency sync (45 min).
2. Role reprioritization tied to product milestones.

### Friday

1. Funnel KPI review (30 min).
2. SLA misses, corrective actions, and next-week commitments.

## Daily standup format (15 min)

1. Candidate movement yesterday.
2. Decisions pending today.
3. Blockers requiring hiring manager or executive action.

## Mandatory SLA

| Stage | SLA |
|---|---|
| Recruiter to HM resume handoff | same day |
| HM resume decision | less than 24h |
| Interview feedback submission | same day |
| Panel debrief decision | less than 24h |
| Offer draft ready | less than 48h |

## Escalation rules

1. Any candidate idle more than 72 hours escalates to recruiting lead.
2. Any critical role open more than 45 days escalates to VP Engineering.
3. Any offer with low confidence close escalates to CTO sponsor.
