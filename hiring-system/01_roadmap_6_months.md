# 6-Month Hiring Execution Roadmap

## Objective

Build and onboard a world-class team to ship a commercial v1 in 6 months.

## Hiring targets by month

| Month | Net New Hires | Primary focus |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 24 | Leadership, core architecture, recruiting engine |
| 2 | 20 | Core engine ICs, QA automation leadership, app security |
| 3 | 22 | Interop, cloud, collaboration, design, docs |
| 4 | 20 | SDK, AI workflows, performance, SRE |
| 5 | 18 | DevRel, docs scale, release quality, support prep |
| 6 | 18 | Final IC backfills, launch readiness, customer support technical |

## Monthly execution checklist

### Month 1

1. Hire VP Engineering, Director Core Engines, Director Platform.
2. Close Principal hires for Geometry, Constraint Solver, Rendering.
3. Hire 2 recruiting sourcers and 1 recruiting coordinator.
4. Build role scorecards for top 15 critical roles.
5. Launch interviewer training and standardized loop.

Exit criteria:

- 70% of P0 roles open and actively sourced.
- 3 principal-level technical hires accepted.
- Interview process SLAs in place and measured.

### Month 2

1. Scale senior IC hiring for kernel, BIM model, parametrics, UI.
2. Hire QA Automation lead and Build systems lead.
3. Onboard first 20 engineers with a 2-week technical bootcamp.
4. Freeze v1 scope and align role priorities.

Exit criteria:

- Architecture spine complete.
- Core modeling alpha plan approved.
- At least 15 accepted offers cumulative.

### Month 3

1. Hire interop specialists (IFC first), cloud/auth team starters.
2. Hire 2 product managers and 2 product designers for pro workflows.
3. Add technical writer and docs infrastructure owner.

Exit criteria:

- End-to-end modeling workflow path staffed.
- IFC quality gate ownership assigned.

### Month 4

1. Hire SDK team core and AI feature team core.
2. Add performance and reliability engineers.
3. Expand QA to automation + regression systems.

Exit criteria:

- Beta candidate release staffing complete.
- SDK beta readiness owner assigned.

### Month 5

1. Hire DevRel engineers and support-side technical specialists.
2. Staff release engineering and launch operations.
3. Run launch simulation staffing drill.

Exit criteria:

- Launch week support staffing plan complete.
- Documentation staffing complete.

### Month 6

1. Fill final critical backfills and close open senior IC reqs.
2. Prepare post-launch staffing model and retention plan.
3. Transition recruiting from hypergrowth to sustain mode.

Exit criteria:

- Launch staffed with on-call coverage.
- 30/60/90 day post-launch hiring plan approved.

## Priority logic

1. Hire decision-makers and architecture owners first.
2. Hire specialists with longest time-to-fill earliest.
3. Build quality and platform teams in parallel with product teams.
4. Delay lower-criticality roles until core execution engine is stable.
