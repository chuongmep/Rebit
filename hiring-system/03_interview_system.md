# Interview System

## Principles

1. High bar and fast decisions can coexist with strict SLAs.
2. Evaluate for role outcomes, not generic puzzle performance.
3. Every panel produces evidence linked to scorecard dimensions.

## Standard interview loop

| Stage | Duration | Owner | Goal |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Recruiter screen | 30 min | Recruiter | Motivation, compensation, logistics |
| Hiring manager technical screen | 60 min | Hiring manager | Domain fit and problem quality |
| Work sample | 2 to 4 h | Candidate + reviewer | Real role signal under realistic constraints |
| Panel round 1 | 60 min | Domain interviewer | Deep technical capability |
| Panel round 2 | 60 min | Cross-functional interviewer | Collaboration and communication |
| Panel round 3 | 45 min | Engineering leader | Ownership and execution judgment |
| Executive close | 30 min | VP Eng or CTO | Mission alignment and close confidence |

## Domain modules

### Rust systems module

- Ownership and lifetimes under real constraints.
- Concurrency safety and determinism.
- Profiling-driven optimization approach.

### Geometry and constraints module

- Robust predicates and tolerance handling.
- Edge-case reasoning and failure containment.
- Solver stability and explainability.

### Rendering and GPU module

- Graphics pipeline architecture decisions.
- Frame-time bottleneck diagnosis.
- GPU memory strategy and debugging workflow.

### Leadership module

- Scope negotiation under deadlines.
- Technical decision tradeoff communication.
- Coaching and raising team quality.

## Decision model

1. Required dimensions scored 1 to 5.
2. Any required dimension below 3 is a no-hire unless VP override with written rationale.
3. Debrief must happen within 24 hours.
4. Written hiring packet stored for audit and calibration.
