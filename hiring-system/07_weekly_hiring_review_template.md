# Weekly Hiring Review Template

## Meeting objective

Ensure priority roles are moving, bottlenecks are resolved, and monthly hiring targets stay on track.

## Inputs required before meeting

1. Updated dashboard CSV.
2. Open role list with age and stage.
3. Offer pipeline and close confidence.
4. SLA exception list.

## Agenda (60 minutes)

1. KPI snapshot (10 min)
2. P0 role deep-dive (20 min)
3. Offer close strategy (10 min)
4. Interview quality and calibration issues (10 min)
5. Decisions and owner assignments (10 min)
